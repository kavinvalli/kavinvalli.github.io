For years my site was a little terminal you had to `type help` into. It was fun,
but I've outgrown it. So I tore it down and rebuilt this place around one thing I
actually want to do more of: **write**.

I used to publish at [LiveCode247](https://livecode247.com). I'm retiring that too. Rather than migrate a pile of old posts, I'm starting from
scratch here. Clean slate.

## What lives here now

- A single paragraph about me, instead of a menu of commands.
- Projects as a plain list — the things I'm proud of, one link each.
- This: **Writing**. Short notes, longer essays, whatever I'm figuring out.

## Why bother

Writing forces me to actually finish a thought. Most of what I learn stays as
half-formed intuition until I try to explain it to someone. This is that someone.

Also, I'm trying to get out of my comfort zone. I want to get started with things I don't know how to do yet. Writing is a good way to do that, because it forces me to learn, explain and stay accountable.

No newsletter, no algorithm. Just a page on the internet that's mine.
